Alexa Holder.

Alexa is a Master of Architecture graduate from the University of Waterloo with experience across architectural practice, research, and professional outreach. She has worked at firms in Canada and abroad, and most recently completed a graduate research and outreach position with the Ontario Association of Architects. Her thesis explored flood resilience and climate adaptation, and was recognized with the Canada Graduate Research Scholarship and OAA Scholarship for Exceptional Leadership Through Design Excellence.
